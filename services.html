Our Hospital Services

We provide a wide range of specialized medical services tailored to meet individual patient needs:

  • General Outpatient Services: Routine consultations and preventative health checkups.
  • Pediatrics & Maternal Care: Specialized care for infants, children, and mothers.
  • Dental Clinic: Comprehensive oral health examinations, cleaning, and procedures.
  • Cardiology & Diagnostics: Advanced diagnostic tools and heart health evaluations.
  • 24/7 Emergency Care: Rapid emergency response and critical care support.
